01 / Process

Support loop

  1. Triage
  2. Reproduce
  3. Observe
  4. Isolate
  5. Investigate
  6. Document
  7. Validate
  8. Hand over

The goal is not to try the most fixes. The goal is to remove uncertainty — one controlled test at a time.
