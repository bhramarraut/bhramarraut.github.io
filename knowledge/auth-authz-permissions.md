# Authentication, Authorization and Permissions

**Authentication:** Verifying who the user is (login, MFA).
**Authorization:** Determining what an authenticated user may do.
**Permissions:** Specific grants tied to roles or policies.
Support issues often cluster around session expiry, wrong role assignment, or missing permission grants.
