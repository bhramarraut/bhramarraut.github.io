# What an SLA Means in Support Operations

An SLA defines expected response or resolution times for support requests, often tied to priority and contract tier.
Understanding SLA helps support teams prioritize work, communicate realistic timelines to customers and recognize when escalation is required to meet commitments.
SLAs are operational agreements — they do not replace good troubleshooting or documentation.
