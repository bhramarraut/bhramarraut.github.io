# What PSM I Changed About How I Think About Scrum

PSM I reinforced empiricism, Scrum accountabilities, events as opportunities for inspection and adaptation, and that Scrum is a framework not a prescriptive methodology.
PSM I ≠ professional Scrum Master employment.
**Source:** [Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/)
