# Incident vs Problem vs Change: A Beginner Mental Model

**Incident:** Unplanned interruption or quality degradation needing restoration of service.
**Problem:** Underlying cause of one or more incidents — focuses on root cause and prevention.
**Change:** Planned addition, modification or removal of something that could affect services.
Fixing an incident restores service; solving a problem reduces repeat incidents.
