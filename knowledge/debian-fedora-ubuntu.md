# Debian vs Fedora vs Ubuntu

**Debian:** Stability and free software purity — great for servers and conservative deployments.
**Fedora:** Fresher packages, upstream innovation — shorter lifecycle.
**Ubuntu:** Balance of convenience, LTS options and ecosystem.
