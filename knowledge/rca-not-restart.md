# Root Cause Analysis: Why "Restarted the App" Is Not a Root Cause

Restarting may restore service temporarily but rarely explains why the failure occurred.
A useful RCA asks: what changed, what dependency failed, what resource was exhausted, what configuration drift occurred?
Document workarounds separately from verified root cause.
